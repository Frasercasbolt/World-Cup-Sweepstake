Rules of the Game

1 · The Teams

The sweepstake runs with six company teams of three, based on a sign-up of eighteen. Entry is £20 a head — £60 per team, every penny into the £360 prize pot. You and your two teammates share one squad, one score, and one destiny.

2 · The Snake Draft

  • The six company teams are ranked 1–6 to set the draft order.
  • Round one runs in order: seed 1 picks first, then 2, 3 … down to seed 6.
  • The order then snakes: seed 6 also opens round two, going back up to seed 1 — and so on, alternating direction each round.
  • Eight rounds in total. 6 company teams × 8 picks = all 48 nations owned. No leftovers, no excuses.

3 · Scoring

Your eight nations earn points for every result and every round they survive. Points are cumulative — a champion banks the whole ladder on the way up.

4 · The Verdict

Highest combined total across your eight nations after the final on 19 July wins. Tie-break: most nations in the knockout rounds, then most group-stage goals scored, then a coin flip overseen by the most neutral person in the office.
